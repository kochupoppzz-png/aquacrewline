Who we are

People first. Professional crew connections.

Aqua Crewline Pvt Ltd is a Kochi, Kerala-based maritime recruitment business focused on connecting seafarers with suitable opportunities across the maritime sector.

We support recruitment across Deck, Engine and General Purpose crew categories, and aim to make communication with candidates and maritime employers clear, responsive and professional.

Our commitment
Trust · Clarity · Opportunity

We value transparent communication, responsible recruitment support and long-term professional relationships in the maritime community.
